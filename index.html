RGV Mobile X-Rays — SMS/Text Messaging Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 15, 2026

Our SMS Program

RGV Mobile X-Rays ("we," "us," "our") sends text messages to patients to confirm receipt of a referral for mobile diagnostic imaging services (X-ray, ultrasound, etc.) ordered by their physician or facility, and to help coordinate scheduling. This policy describes how we handle the phone number and related information used for that texting program.

These text messages do not contain protected health information (diagnosis, treatment details, or exam results). They are limited to confirming that a referral was received and providing a phone number to call for scheduling.

How We Obtain Your Number and Consent

Your phone number is provided to us by the physician's office or facility that referred you for imaging services, as part of that existing referral relationship — not collected through a website sign-up form. You may opt out of receiving texts at any time (see below).

Information We Collect

  • Your name and phone number, as provided by the referring physician/facility
  • The content and delivery status of messages we send you
  • Your opt-out status, if you reply STOP

How We Use This Information

Solely to send you the referral-confirmation and scheduling-related text messages described above, and to honor opt-out requests.

How We Share This Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in/consent information with any third party for marketing or promotional purposes. We share this information only with:

  • Our SMS delivery provider (currently Twilio), solely to transmit the messages on our behalf
  • Others, if required by law

Message Frequency & Costs

Message frequency varies based on your referral activity. Message and data rates may apply, per your mobile carrier plan.

Opting Out

Reply STOP to any message at any time to stop receiving texts from us. Reply HELP for assistance. You can also call us directly at 956-783-9779.

Terms and Conditions

See our Terms and Conditions for the full rules of this messaging program.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information described in this policy from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or our text messaging program: call 956-783-9779.
