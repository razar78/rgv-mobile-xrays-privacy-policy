Program Description

RGV Mobile X-Rays ("we," "us," "our") operates an SMS text messaging program to notify patients that a referral for mobile diagnostic imaging services (X-ray, ultrasound, etc.) has been received from their physician or facility, and to assist with scheduling. By participating in this program (i.e., by not opting out after receiving a message), you agree to these terms.

Eligibility & How You're Added

Phone numbers are provided to us by the physician's office or facility that referred you for services, as part of that existing referral relationship. Messages are not sent based on a website opt-in form. You must be the account holder of the mobile number, or have the account holder's permission, to receive messages at that number.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your referral activity — typically a small number of messages around the time a referral is received. This is not a recurring marketing program.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, per your mobile carrier plan. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Opting Out & Help

Reply STOP to any message at any time to stop receiving texts from us. Reply HELP for assistance. You can also call us directly at 956-783-9779.
